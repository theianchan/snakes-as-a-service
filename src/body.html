Boxwood Tactics

Maren Boxwood left her company a letter, a guest list, and one last job: lay a long table at the ford and invite everyone.

How to play
  • Each unit may move and act once per turn, in either order.
  • High ground adds damage to arrows and spells.
  • A blow from behind deals half again as much.
  • A stilled unit cannot move on its next turn.
  • Doing something for the first time earns extra experience.
  • Swipe sideways to turn the map. Tap anyone to size them up.
