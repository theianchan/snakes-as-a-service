Last Call Tactics

Level 1 · A rooftop in the Mission

The fog wasn't invited.

Cocktail night is going well until the marine layer rolls over Twin Peaks and comes for the ice. Clear the roof before last call.

  • Tap a blue tile to move, then pick an action. Each crew member gets one move and one action per turn.
  • Hit from behind for +50% damage, from the side for +25%. Choose your facing when you end a turn.
  • High ground adds damage to thrown and ranged attacks.
  • Drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap anyone to size them up.
