Bottled
Level 1 · The Last Word, 2 a.m.

Last Call Tactics

The bottles on the back bar have started to rattle. Every spirit in the house wants out, and the closing crew is all that stands between them and the street. Put them back.

  • Soak, then light. Soaked targets take double from fire and flare onto whoever is next to them. That includes your crew.
  • Use the room. Shove spirits off the bar, into walls, into each other, or into a keg.
  • Mind your back. Hits from behind deal +50%. You choose a facing when a turn ends.
